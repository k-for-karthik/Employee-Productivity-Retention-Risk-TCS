# 🧑‍💻 Employee Productivity & Retention Risk – TCS

[You can access the project files here](https://github.com/k-for-karthik/Employee-Productivity-Retention-Risk-TCS)<br>
### 🧠 Business Problem
TCS observed inconsistencies in productivity and attendance across roles and departments. Leadership needed data-driven insights to address burnout, absenteeism, and potential attrition.

### 📊 Data & Tools Used
- **Data Size:** 25,575 cleaned employee activity records
- **Tools:** `SQL`, `Excel`

### 🔍 Approach
1. Cleaned and prepared raw HR and productivity data by removing duplicates, fixing nulls, and validating metrics.
2. Analyzed productivity scores by work mode (office, remote, hybrid).
3. Identified patterns in absenteeism, overtime, and missed deadlines.
4. Performed retention risk assessment based on productivity and attendance indicators.

### 💡 Key Findings
- **Hybrid workers** were the most productive (avg. score 74.88).
- **Customer Support** had the highest absenteeism rate (10.48%).
- Productivity dropped significantly on **Fridays** and with **excessive overtime**.
- **468 employees** identified as high retention risk, especially WFH and medium salary band roles.

### 📈 Impact & Outcome
- Recommendations led to **10%+ improvement in task completion**.
- Enabled targeted retention plans and engagement initiatives.
- Supported hybrid work model expansion for higher productivity.
